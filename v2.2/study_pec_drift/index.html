PEC Drift — When to Learn, When to Apply¶

Branch: feature/equatorial_mode (Oct 2026). Started from 2026-10-20 20:45, when PEC Drift Correction learnt PHD2's catch-up after guiding started before tracking as a -30"/min Dec drift, applied it for 20 min (-165"), and PHD2 spent those minutes pulsing it back. This study sets out which guide corrections PEC should learn from, which it should not (ignore cases), when the rate it has learnt is fit to apply (inhibit cases), how each case is detected for both sync guiding and pulse guiding, and the data that verifies the design.

Code: driver/control_pec.py (PecMixin, PecAxis, GuiderCalibrationDetector). Analysis: utility/analyse_tracking.ipynb section 3 (who corrected the drift), utility/analyse_sessions.ipynb.

Terms¶

  • Drift: the slowly varying rate at which the sky moves against the mount after the worm feed-forward, per axis (RA, Dec). PEC learns it from the running total of guide corrections, y(t) = pulse guiding + sync guiding + PEC applied (correction_breakdown), as an EMA of the rate over pec_tau_sec (450 s).
  • Learn: fold a guide correction into the rate. Ignore = take the correction into the total (it really was a correction) but not into the rate, keep applying the rate already learnt, and restart the rate's differencing afterwards (PecAxis.hold). An ignore case says why a correction isn't evidence of drift; it can last for one correction (STEP) or for a span of them (REVERSAL, ACQUIRE).
  • Discard = drop the correction altogether, not even into the total (OUTLIER today). Kept for corrections that can't be real; dropping a real one leaves the total short by it from then on.
  • Apply: feed the learnt rate forward to the PID every tick. Inhibit = don't apply it (the axis's rate isn't trusted yet, or no longer). An inhibit case says why.
  • Reset: throw the model away (a new pointing). Not an ignore: nothing learnt before it is kept.

The two lists below are the names logged in PECLOG: inhibit (per axis, today) and, proposed, ignore (the ignore case, e.g. 'REVERSAL:M2', 'ACQUIRE'; it replaces today's paused, which carries only the motors of a reversal or slack crossing, e.g. 'M2' — the notebooks read both, for older logs).

Requirements¶

  • R1 Learn only drift. A correction that measures anything else (a re-centre, a guider's deliberate move, a mechanical transient, a stale position) is ignored, whichever guider sent it.
  • R2 One design for sync and pulse guiding. Both reduce to y(t) per axis. Thresholds are rates (correction ÷ update interval) or are scaled by each axis's own update interval: a 5 min sync gap with a 60" residual and a 2 s pulse of 1" are the same 12"/min.
  • R3 Use what the driver knows. Events the driver sees exactly (tracking on, goto/rotate complete, a pulse it rejected, a motor reversal, BLC acting, stale 518 data) start an ignore case directly, without waiting for the signal.
  • R4 Judge bursts over time, not per update. A catch-up, a dither or a slack crossing spans many pulses; a rule that sees one update at a time either misses it or, after a few in a row, learns it.
  • R5 Bounded damage. Whatever the ignore cases miss moves the rate only so far per update (robust update), so one missed case can't fit -30"/min.
  • R6 Undo what was learnt in hindsight. A case recognised only after it started (a calibration on its 3rd pulse, a burst once it is clear) rolls the model back to where the case began.
  • R7 Don't hide real drift. A genuine change of drift (a flip, a new target, cable drag) is still learnt within about τ; corrections ignored on clean guiding cost little learning time.
  • R8 Apply only a trustworthy rate. The rate is applied once it rests on enough time and corrections, not merely enough corrections (3 pulses arrive in 4 s).
  • R9 Explain itself. Each ignore and inhibit is logged with its case name (PECLOG ignore, inhibit) and drawn by the analysis notebooks.
  • R10 Testable offline and closed loop. Every case has archived examples and a digital twin scenario.

Detection design¶

Three layers, applied per axis to y(t):

  1. Event gates (R3). The driver's events start ignore cases directly: ACQUIRE, CATCHUP, GAP, REVERSAL, SLACK, SLIP, BLC, STALL, NONSIDEREAL, WORMTEST, JOG. Each has a minimum time; release also needs the burst test to report the axis settled. Cheap and exact, and they cover the cases seen so far that matter most.
  2. Signal tests (R2, R4), all on rates scaled by the axis's own update interval:
    • burst test: the rate over a short window (3 updates or 30 s, whichever is longer) against the model's rate and its σ; one-sided and beyond kσ is BURST, and its "back within kσ" is the settled signal for every case that spans corrections;
    • saturation (PG): SATURATED;
    • calibration, per axis: CALIBRATEPHD2, CALIBRATECCD;
    • step and outlier, as rates: STEP, OUTLIER.
  3. Robust update (R5). Before each update's observed rate enters the EMA it is clipped to the model's rate ± kσ (Huber-style). A missed case can then move the rate by at most kσ x α per update; a real change of drift still gets through over a few updates (R7).

Rollback (R6): each ignore case that is recognised late snapshots the model at its start (as the calibration detector does today with run_start) and restores it when confirmed: CATCHUP from the first rejected pulse, BURST from its first update, CALIBRATE* from the run's start.

Logging (R9): PECLOG ignore (replacing paused) carries the case name and, for mechanical cases, the motor ('REVERSAL:M2', 'SLACK:M3'); inhibit per axis as today plus MIN_SPAN / UNCERTAIN / STALE. A ->> Polaris: PEC drift ignoring corrections (CATCHUP) / ... learning again line at each change (today: PEC drift learning paused / resumed). The notebook shades each case in section 3, reading ignore or, on older logs, paused.

Verification¶

Data we have¶

  • 76 archived sessions (utility/sessions.toml): 45 sync guiding, 11 pulse guiding (PHD2), 12 both, 8 unguided. PECLOG on all of them; KFLOG/PIDLOG (log_position) on some (2026-10-20 the first with BLC state).
  • PHD2 guide logs for most pulse-guided sessions (load_phd2_guidelog): calibrations, dithers, star lost, the guider's own error and pulses.
  • The mount-drift total is nearly independent of PEC: y(t) includes what PEC applied, so the same drift is seen whatever PEC did. Gate designs can therefore be replayed open loop on the logged y(t) (pe_analysis.replay_pec_rate).
  • Ground truth for replay: the centred ±5 min drift rate (pe_analysis.true_rate), with windows around known events left out.
  • Digital twin, closed loop: tests/sim_guider.py — SimGuider (PG, PHD2-like: frame time, aggression, guide rate) and SimPlateSolver (SG: interval, solve noise), with drift and worm, already used by tests/test_pec_twin_drift.py and test_pec_twin_worm_ff.py. The only place the PEC-vs-guider fight itself shows.
  • Live: utility/replay/replay.py replays a recorded request sequence against the driver on nina01.

Plan¶

  1. Event catalogue (read-only): from the archive, every instance of each ignore case — driver log lines (goto, tracking, rotate, Cannot pulse guide, BACKLASH, reversals, PECLOG step/stale, lag warnings, restarts), PHD2 events (calibration, dither, star lost), m2_slips. Gives each case's frequency, a test set per case, and the clean stretches for measuring corrections ignored needlessly.
  2. Baseline score of today's PEC on the catalogue: replay and score (below).
  3. Add the layers one at a time (events, signal tests, robust update), each scored on the replay and given a twin scenario: guiding before tracking (CATCHUP), a PHD2 calibration (CALIBRATEPHD2), dithers every 2 min (BURST), star lost for 60 s (GAP), bad solves (STEP), an M2 slip (SLIP), a reversal (REVERSAL), a goto then sync guiding (ACQUIRE), and a genuine step change of drift (R7).
  4. Live check on nina01 for the cases the twin can't model well (CATCHUP with PHD2, SATURATED).

Scores and acceptance criteria (proposed)¶

  • Rate error after a case: the learnt rate within 2"/min of the truth throughout and after each case instance.
  • No fight: over any 10 min, the guider's and PEC's totals don't oppose each other by more than 30" (today's PEC undone by guider % in the notebook, windowed).
  • Needless ignores: on clean stretches, ≤ 5% of learning time lost.
  • Real drift still learnt (R7): a step change of drift reaches 63% of its new value within 1.5 τ (twin).
  • No regression: on every session class, replay rate error rms no worse than today's.

Evidence so far¶

  • 2026-09-29 PHD2 calibration learnt as an RA drift of -785"/h, guiding then fought it at +363"/min → CALIBRATEPHD2 (built).
  • 2026-10-11 SMC: M2's reversal at 21:19, the guider's pull-back learnt as drift → REVERSAL (built).
  • 2026-10-17 22:42 PHD2 stopped with the rate at -12.8"/min, applied for 12.6 min (-161" RA) → STALE (built); the restart after it → GAP (proposed).
  • 2026-10-17 23:27 a +165" Dec sync then -185": +22"/min learnt → STEP (built).
  • 2026-10-18 20:36 M2 at 0.24"/s crossing its 432" slack after a goto under 6 s syncs: 50'/h learnt, fought for 30 min → SLACK (built).
  • 2026-10-19 20:37-20:43 M3's reversal under PHD2 with BLC latched: 5-15" kicks at each push, Dec to 22" → REVERSAL ignored the corrections; BLC no longer compensates reversals (d9f53c5a).
  • 2026-10-20 20:45 PHD2 guiding before tracking: 36 pulses rejected, then -37.5" Dec pulses every 3-5 s; VALID after 10 s at R² 1.0, -30"/min learnt, -165" applied by 20:58 while PHD2 pulsed from -239" back to +48" → CATCHUP, SATURATED, MIN_SPAN (proposed). The calibration detector missed the repeated pulses: RA pulses in between reset its run (→ per-axis runs).

Open questions¶

  • The burst test's window and k for sync guiding with long intervals (2-5 min): enough updates to judge?
  • SLIP live: can the driver see a slip in time from the 518 angles alone (m2_slips works offline on PECLOG)?
  • NONSIDEREAL: are pulses learnt while tracking an orbital today?
  • Rollback depth: how far back can CATCHUP safely restore (the model before the rejected pulses may itself be from a previous target)?
  • Should ACQUIRE also cover the first minutes after a driver restart with tracking restored?
  • OUTLIER: discard (today) or ignore? Discarding a real correction leaves the total short by it from then on; ignoring keeps the total right but needs the threshold to be sure it isn't drift.
