# Troubleshooting
Choose the guide for your problem. Each problem has a code, such as A2 or C3, and its letter shows which guide it's in.

* **[Installation](./troubleshooting-install.md)** (A codes): installing, starting or updating the Alpaca Driver, including setup.bat, uv and Python packages, Windows security warnings, opening Alpaca Pilot and starting the driver at boot.
* **[Benro Polaris](./troubleshooting-polaris.md)** (B codes): the Benro Polaris itself and the Benro app, including starting and charging the Polaris, its Wi-Fi and password, tracking quality and star alignment.
* **[Network](./troubleshooting-network.md)** (C codes): the Wi-Fi connection between the driver's computer and the Benro Polaris, including reading the driver's log, connection dropouts, reaching Alpaca Pilot at ap.local, and diagnosing the network with PuTTY and PowerShell.
* **[NINA](./troubleshooting-nina.md)** (N codes): using NINA with the Alpaca Driver, including remote access to a MiniPC running NINA, choosing the mount, autofocus, plate solving and connecting cameras.
* **[Stellarium](./troubleshooting-stellarium.md)** (S codes): connecting Stellarium PLUS and Stellarium Desktop to the Alpaca Driver, and using Stellarium over Remote Desktop.

If your problem isn't covered, check the [FAQ](./faq.md).
